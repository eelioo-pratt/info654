Life Experiences

They say wisdom comes with age and experience...but I call that a load of malarkey! I am the same as everyone else, just trying to figure things out and grow along the way. My wife, god rest her soul, always seemed to be one step ahead of me, in life, a career, and even as a parent. However, I believe she was just better at hiding her insecurities, and much better at adapting to her environment. As we both aged, and she faded away, I made a promise to strive for a better understanding of the world around me, and to live as she did... all the way till the end. Below you will find some things that have helped me on my journey.

- Great Sand Dunes National Park and Preserve is a place where I go to meditate and connect with my higher power. I truly feel at peace here, and everything seems to feel calm... at least until tourist season.

- Breckenridge Mountain is where I learned to snowboard. Some people say that a man of my age shouldn't be so reckless, but to that I say, why not? Learning to ski or snowboard was always something I had dreamed of, but never got around to it. A few years back I decided that I couldn't wait any longer, and that winter I laced up my boots, strapped on my helmet, and threw my self down the mountain.

- Moab Utah was my wife's happy place. We would visit every year, soak up the sun, enjoy the scenic trails, and cuddle up under the stars. I miss those days... but I still go back every year, for her.

- Fairplay Colorado is a small town I call home. It is a quiet community, full of assorted mountain people, who all moved here for the same reason I did... to be one with nature. Although, I recently learned that some of the younger folk are moving out here because of some silly kids cartoon called South Park, which apparently is based off of Fairplay. I tried watching it, but didn't like it. Oh well, to each their own, I suppose.
